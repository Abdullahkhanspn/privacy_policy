Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 2026

Welcome to Snap & Cook ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards your information when you use our application.

Summary: Snap & Cook uses your camera/photos to recognize cooking ingredients using Artificial Intelligence (Google Gemini AI) and recommend recipes. We respect your privacy and do not sell your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide and improve our AI recipe recommendation services:

  • Personal Account Information: When you register or log in using Email or Google Authentication, we collect your name, email address, profile picture URL, and unique user ID (UID).
  • Images & Camera Data: Photos of food items and ingredients you capture or upload from your gallery are processed to recognize ingredients and recommend recipes.
  • Profile & Dietary Preferences: Preferences such as dietary restrictions, food allergies, skill level, and saved recipes that you configure in your profile.
  • Usage & Device Info: Basic device technical information required for app operations, authentication, and error logging.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • To identify ingredients from uploaded photos using AI image recognition models.
  • To generate personalized cooking recipes based on available ingredients and dietary preferences.
  • To provide safety warnings regarding food allergies configured in your user profile.
  • To manage your account, store saved recipes, and keep your user preferences synced.
  • To improve app functionality, performance, and user experience.

3. Third-Party Services & AI Processing

We integrate trusted third-party services to deliver core app functionality:

  • Google Firebase: Used for secure user authentication (Firebase Auth), database management (Cloud Firestore), and file storage (Firebase Storage).
  • Google Generative AI (Gemini API): Ingredient images and dietary context are sent securely to Google's Gemini AI to generate recipe suggestions.
  • Google Sign-In: Allows secure single sign-on authentication.

4. Permissions Requested

The app requires the following device permissions to function properly:

  • Camera Permission: To take real-time photos of ingredients.
  • Photo Library / Storage Permission: To select ingredient images saved on your device.
  • Internet Permission: To connect to Firebase services and fetch AI-generated recipes.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your profile data as long as your account remains active. You can request account and data deletion at any time by logging into the app and deleting your profile, or by contacting our support team at support@snapandcook.com.

6. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can promptly delete it.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted within the app along with an updated effective date.
